Season / Position are the controls (Load-button pattern, matches ADP Comparison / NFL Stats -- no auto-fetch on change, no auto-load on tab open). No Scoring control yet -- see app.py's own comment above the /playercompare routes: everything here is PPR-based until a per-format leaderboard/percentile aggregation exists to select between. The leaderboard (/playercompare/data) streams in on Load; checking 2+ players there and clicking "Compare selected" streams in the comparison charts (/playercompare/compare) below it. `#playercompare-charts` is a SIBLING of the controls/leaderboard
below, NOT nested inside it -- a card nested inside another card still reads as one long panel (the outer card's border/padding wraps both), which is what "completely separated" here means to fix: the comparison results get their own independent card at the top level of this fragment, not a card-in-a-card. #}

Compare same-position players against the real-NFL field, and against each other week by week — built for lineup decisions, not a season-end leaderboard. No league required.

{# `nfl-apply playercompare-load`: keeps .nfl-apply's shared green button styling but overrides its `margin-left: auto` (style.css) with a scoped rule -- Load sits with the Position/Season criteria on the LEFT here, not pushed to the row's right edge the way NFL Stats/ADP's own Load buttons are (per user request; those other tabs are untouched, since .nfl-apply itself isn't). #} {# Compare selected + Clear selection stay RIGHT-JUSTIFIED as a pair (user request) -- Compare selected's OWN `margin-left: auto` (style.css) pushes it and everything after it in source order (Clear) to the row's right edge, while Load/the two selects keep normal left-to-right flow. They also appear/disappear TOGETHER, both hidden until 2+ rows are checked -- never one without the other. Showing Clear alone as soon as 1 row is checked (an earlier design) meant it could pop in and back out on every single checkbox click while exactly 1 was checked, a visible "jiggle" in the row's width; gating both on the SAME "2+ checked" condition removes that in-between state entirely, so nothing in this row ever appears/disappears except this one pair, at one shared moment. #} {# Unchecks every currently-picked row in one click -- swapping out an earlier selection for a fresh pair used to mean scrolling the board to find and manually uncheck each one. Deliberately does NOT touch #playercompare-charts -- the previous comparison stays visible below until a new one is actually run, rather than blanking the page the moment Clear is clicked (see wireCheckboxes()'s own comment on why). #}
