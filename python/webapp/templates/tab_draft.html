Each column is a manager's draft slot, each row a round. The first number under a pick is the points that player put up on the drafting team's roster this season, started or benched; the second (in parens) is his TRUE full season output — every real stat line he had, regardless of who (if anyone) rostered or started him — if that differs. Pos #N is where he finished at his position leaguewide this season, from that same true-season number — colour marks the position. Points are tinted green when the player's true season value beat his draft slot by a full round or more and red when it missed by that much; anything closer to expectation is left unhighlighted.
